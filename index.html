Core subsystems

Private by design, with you in the loop.

01

Local encrypted vault

Credentials live in an on-device IndexedDB vault protected by AES-256-GCM and a PBKDF2 passphrase key. No cloud account required.

02

Portal profiles

Save a sign-in for one exact site origin. GhostState checks scheme, hostname and port before it ever fills a field.

03

Learned sign-in steps

Capture multi-step login flows as versioned workflows. You review and approve each fill, and submit forms yourself.

04

Companion transfer

Move credentials to a nearby device with an explicitly approved, encrypted transfer. No cookies or active sessions are copied.
